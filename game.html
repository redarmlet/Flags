HOW TO PLAY

In this game, you have 3s to recognize each flag from your selected pool.



ATTEMPTS:

You can try up to 2 times per flag. Failing the second time will cost you 1 life (out of 3 total).



THE BOOK:

You can open it to check the list of all possible nations.

CONTROLS

1. Click the RED BUTTON or press 8 to start a new game.

2. Type your answer and press ENTER to check.

3. Click the BLUE BUTTON or press 1 to toggle the book.

4. Click the top/left icon to open the menu or toggle pressing 2.

5. Press 0 to toggle music.

6. Press 9 to toggle sound effects

7. Press ESC to open the main menu, or close any menu.
